Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

The Kriplika publisher posts content on behalf of a single account owner. It does not collect, store or share personal data about anyone else. It does not read followers, comments or messages.

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to publish the owner's posts. Images are hosted here for the platforms to download at posting time.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/marker2601/kriplika-media/issues.
